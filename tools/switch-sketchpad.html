SWITCH SKETCHPAD

Draw one switch the way it should look, at the board's real pixel size. Your drawing becomes the switch in the game.

  1. Pick a color and paint on the grid. Click or drag to paint; right-click or the Eraser clears.
  2. Draw the switch leaning left with a ball resting on it. The game flips it for the other direction, and drops the ball pixels for an empty switch.
  3. The faint lines are the channel walls and the pivot line, exactly where they sit on the board. The ghost circles show where a falling ball passes in each channel.
  4. When the preview looks right, press Copy drawing and paste it into the chat.

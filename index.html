GOALS AND EXIT VALUES

Exit values mirror left and right, so set them from the centre out to the edge. Negative values are allowed.

Changes here take effect when you apply them, which starts a new game.
HOW TO PLAY
  • Take turns dropping a ball into one of the 8 columns. Tap a column or press 1–8. Pick an opponent to play a friend on this screen or the computer.
  • Each switch has two sides: a thick slope that holds a ball and a flat bar that lets one through. A ball that lands on an empty slope stops there and your turn ends.
  • A ball that hits the flat bar flips the switch and keeps falling.
  • A ball that hits a loaded switch from either side knocks the resting ball loose, and both fall to the next level. One ball in, two balls out: that's how chain reactions build.
  • You score every ball that reaches an exit during your turn. Exits further from the middle are worth more in rounds 2–4.
  • Once a player reaches the round's goal, the other player gets one last turn. Reaching the goal earns it again as a bonus, and the round's winner takes the point difference from the loser.
  • The board carries over between rounds, so a quiet round is a chance to load it up for the next one.
  • Press Pass (or −) to skip a turn when you don't like the board. With the pass limit on, you get three per half and can't pass twice in a row.
  • Lowball (in Options) flips the goal: the lower total wins.
